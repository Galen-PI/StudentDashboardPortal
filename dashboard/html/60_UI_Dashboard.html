How scoring works

Each student gets a score from 0–100. Higher means more at risk. Points from multiple factors add together, weighted so that graduation timeline risk always dominates.

Needs Attention — Score 60 or above. Immediate action needed.
Watch Closely — Score 30–59. Monitor this week.
On Track — Score 0–29. No major concerns.
No Data Yet — No academic, trades, or time data found.
Academic Factors and Weekly Intervention Report Factors share ONE capped group (WIR is strictly an academic signal — it carries no weight toward Trades). Each Trade's own factors are capped separately per trade. Every category is capped at 45 points as a group — several checks in the same category often describe one real underlying problem, so one bad category alone gets a student close to "Needs Attention" but not there by itself. Crossing into "Needs Attention" generally means more than one category has real issues, or an individual factor already at or above 45 (e.g. CRITICAL admin priority) on its own. Hover over a student's score to see exactly which factors added up to it.
Academic Factors
+40 Student is flagged as at-risk pace in Edgenuity
+30 Student is behind or slow on academic pace
+25 Graduation deadline is within 30 days and less than 90% complete
+20 Academic completion is below 50%
+15 No academic hours logged this week
+25 No hours logged this week, but periods were scheduled (used instead of the +15 above when schedule data exists)
+15 Logged less than half of scheduled academic periods this week
Trades Factors (capped per trade)
+25 Very low trade progress (under 25%) with less than 60 days to ETAR
+20 Pace gap is more than 10% behind
+15 Trade program status is inactive or withdrawn
+25 Stalled for 2 consecutive months (non-BCT, below 3% gain)
+15 Low monthly gain — below 3% in the most recent month (non-BCT)
+15 BCT: stalled for 2 consecutive months below 1.5% gain
+10 BCT: low monthly gain below 1.5% this month
+10 Stalled or low gain AND overall completion still under 25%
Weekly Intervention Report Factors (academic only — shares the Academic Factors cap above, no weight toward Trades)
+40 Admin priority is CRITICAL this week
+25 Admin priority is HIGH this week
+20 Urgency is IMMEDIATE
+15 Student has an open case
+10 Admin priority is MEDIUM / urgency is URGENT
Time & Data Factors (only one of these three ever applies)
+20 Less than 1 total hour logged
+20 No academic or trades data found at all
+10 No time data found (but other data exists)

Trade Complete students are excluded from trades risk flags. BCT uses a lower monthly gain threshold (1.5%). A score of 0 with real data on file is still On Track — No Data Yet means no data exists at all. Max score is 100.

Generate Academic Snapshot

Pulls every active student's current remaining credits directly from Student Course Data, compares it to their last recorded snapshot of the same type, and writes today's numbers to Academic Snapshots. Run this on Fridays (weekly) or the last day of the month (monthly).

Generate Trade Snapshot

Pulls every active student's current trade completion percentage directly from Trade Overview (kept current by ETAR uploads), compares it to their last recorded snapshot of the same type and same trade, and writes today's numbers to Trade Snapshots. Run this on Fridays (weekly) or the last day of the month (monthly).
