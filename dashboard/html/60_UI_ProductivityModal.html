Plain-language explanations of the numbers shown on the Individual Student tab.
Overall Time Used
Total Hours Worked ÷ Total Scheduled, across every week tracked. It's the same idea as each week's individual percentage, just added up over the whole tracked period instead of one week at a time. 70% and above is generally considered on track; below 40% is worth a closer look.
Total Hours Worked
The sum of academic hours this student actually logged in Edgenuity, added up across every week tracked.
Total Scheduled
The sum of academic hours this student was scheduled for, added up across every week tracked. This comes from whatever weekly schedule was uploaded (or estimated from a pattern, if that week's schedule wasn't uploaded yet).
Weeks Tracked
How many weeks actually count toward the numbers above. A week only counts if the student had scheduled academic hours that week — weeks spent entirely in Trades, or weeks with no schedule on file at all, are left out since there's nothing meaningful to measure. A week marked "Excluded" (see the Audit column on this tab) is also left out.
Suggested Weekly Hours
A recommended weekly scheduled-hours target, based on how many hours this student actually tends to complete — weighted toward their most recent weeks, so a recent improvement or slump counts more than something from months ago. Use it as a more realistic pacing target than a flat default number.
Usage (in the week-by-week table below)
That single week's worked-vs-scheduled percentage — the same math as Overall Time Used, just for one week instead of the whole period.
vs. Group
How this student's usage that week compares to the average usage across everyone else tracked that same week. Green means meaningfully above average, red means meaningfully below, and "About avg" means it's within a few points either way.
Audit / Exclude
Marks a specific week as excluded from every number on this page — for weeks a student was sick, had an appointment, or worked on their high school outside their actual scheduled class time in a way that throws off that week's percentage. The week still shows in the table with a reason attached, it's just left out of the math.
